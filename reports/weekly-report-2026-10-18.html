Analyst Notes
Ecommerce & Conversion
Working
Brand net sales more than doubled versus last year to $248,621, driven by a 116% surge in sessions — paid social alone accounts for nearly half of all traffic at 47%, which is pulling the overall mix heavily toward top-of-funnel new visitors.
The store is punching well above last year, up 168% to $19,960 with a higher average order value of $161, suggesting the in-person experience is converting well when customers show up.
Needs Attention
Conversion rate came in at 2.40% against a forecast of 3.0% and last year's 2.92% — with paid social flooding the site with new, less purchase-ready visitors, the gap is understandable but worth addressing through stronger on-site landing experiences.
The BFF+ is the top-selling product with 778 units sold but only 2.7 weeks of stock cover remaining, which is the tightest position in the range and a real risk if this week's sales pace continues.
This week: Flag the BFF+ stock cover urgently with the buying or ops team and confirm whether a replenishment order is already in motion to avoid a stockout within the next fortnight.
Paid Media
Working
Paid media is delivering well above expectations — return on ad spend hit 5.1x against a forecast of 3.0x, and cost per order dropped to $30 versus a forecast of $65, meaning the team is getting significantly more revenue for every dollar spent than planned.
The two BFF and BFF+ Shopping campaigns together spent around $3,300 and are likely contributing meaningfully to the top product performance, with product-specific Shopping campaigns helping capture high-intent buyers searching for the new launch.
Needs Attention
Google spend has nearly quadrupled versus last year to $8,341, with PMAX and Shopping prospecting taking the largest shares — it is worth checking whether the PMAX campaign is cannibalising the branded and Shopping campaigns rather than finding genuinely incremental customers.
Paid social is driving 47% of all sessions, which creates a heavy reliance on Meta for volume — if that channel has a disruption or costs rise, the whole traffic base is exposed, so building other channels alongside it matters.
This week: Pull the Google PMAX campaign's search term and asset group breakdown this week to check whether it is overlapping with branded and Shopping activity, and adjust exclusions if needed.
Retention & Revenue
Working
New customer acquisition is tracking ahead of plan at 66.9% versus a forecast of 65%, which reflects the paid social and launch activity doing its job of bringing fresh buyers into the brand.
Average order value of $150 online is comfortably ahead of last year's $129 and above the $140 forecast, suggesting customers are either buying more items per order or choosing higher-priced products — both are positive signs for basket health.
Needs Attention
The two Matcha launch email campaigns generated modest revenue — $1,303 and $313 respectively — despite strong open rates of 46% and 60%, which points to engaged subscribers who are not yet converting, possibly because the Matcha range is new and needs more social proof or a clearer call to action.
The return rate has risen to 3.4% from 2.6% last year, with return value more than doubling to $9,180 — given the BFF+ is a new product driving the bulk of volume, it is worth checking whether returns are concentrated there as customers size or style-test a product they haven't bought before.
This week: Pull a returns breakdown by product this week to see whether the BFF+ is driving the rate increase, and if so, consider adding a fit guide or size confidence message to its product page and post-purchase emails.
