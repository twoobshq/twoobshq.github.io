Analyst Notes
Ecommerce & Conversion
Working
Paid social is doing the heavy lifting, driving half of all sessions and contributing to a 111% year-on-year lift in traffic that has pushed online sales to $188,135 — more than double last year.
Average order value of $154 is running well above forecast ($140) and last year ($118), suggesting customers are buying more confidently, possibly driven by the BFF+ launch adding a higher-priced option to the mix.
Needs Attention
Conversion rate of 2.80% is below both last year (3.11%) and forecast (3.0%), which is worth watching given the volume of new traffic — 70.9% of buyers are new customers, and first-time visitors typically convert at lower rates, so some gap is expected but it should be monitored.
Organic search and organic social combined account for only 9% of sessions, which is a thin foundation of owned traffic for a brand growing this quickly — if paid spend were to pull back, there is limited organic volume to fall back on.
This week: Run a quick audit of the top landing pages receiving paid social traffic to check load speed and mobile experience, as improving on-page conversion for new visitors is the fastest lever to close the gap back to the 3.0% forecast.
Paid Media
Working
Overall paid media is performing exceptionally well — return on ad spend of 6.2x is nearly double last year's 3.2x and well ahead of the 3.0x forecast, while cost per order has dropped to $25 against a $65 forecast, meaning the business is acquiring customers far more efficiently than planned.
Meta is carrying the bulk of spend at $24,266 and appears to be the primary engine behind the paid social channel that drove 21,732 sessions this week, suggesting current creative or audience targeting is resonating strongly.
Needs Attention
The Demand Gen campaign for The Seeker spent only $183 this week, which is a very modest investment for a product that sold 100 units and has healthy stock cover of nearly 15 weeks — there may be room to scale this campaign while the product has momentum.
Brand and branded search campaigns (Shopping Brand at $909 and Search Branded at $619) are together spending $1,528 to capture people already looking for TWOOBS — with new customer rate at 70.9%, it is worth confirming these are not cannibalising organic brand traffic that would convert anyway.
This week: Increase the daily budget on the Demand Gen Seeker campaign by at least 50% this week to capitalise on current sell-through momentum before it softens.
Retention & Revenue
Working
The Sneaky Jane relaunch email sent on 25 August is still generating revenue ($3,688 recorded this week) with a standout 55.6% open rate, well above typical benchmarks, which points to a highly engaged subscriber list and strong appetite for restock announcements.
The return rate has dropped to 2.4% from 4.7% last year on a much larger order volume, which suggests customers are finding the right fit or style more reliably — a positive signal for product satisfaction and retention long-term.
Needs Attention
At 70.9% new customers this week — well above the 65% forecast and a significant jump from last year's 50.2% — the returning customer base is not growing proportionally with overall sales, which means there is a meaningful gap in post-purchase nurture to convert this week's new buyers into repeat customers.
Email drove only 1,642 sessions (4% of total traffic) this week, which is a small contribution relative to the size of a list that can achieve 55.6% open rates — there may be an opportunity to send more campaigns or flows to an audience that is clearly willing to engage.
This week: Set up or review the post-purchase email flow this week to ensure every new customer from this high-volume period receives a timely second-purchase prompt within 14 to 21 days of their order.
