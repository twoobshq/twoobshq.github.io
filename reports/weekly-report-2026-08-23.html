Analyst Notes
Ecommerce & Conversion
Working
Paid social is doing the heavy lifting at 43% of all sessions, and the site is converting that traffic well — online conversion rate of 2.71% is 65% better than last year, even if it sits just under the 3.0% forecast.
Store sales are a standout this week, more than quadrupling last year's result to $12,332, with a higher average order value of $160 suggesting customers are buying with confidence in person.
Needs Attention
Organic search is contributing only 7% of sessions with no year-on-year context available, which is worth watching — if paid social ever pulls back, there is not much organic cushion to catch the fall.
Email is driving just 5% of sessions, which feels low given the size of the send activity this week — the Seeker campaigns do not appear to be moving significant traffic volume through to the site.
This week: Run a quick audit of the top organic search landing pages to identify any that have dropped in ranking and could be refreshed to build a stronger non-paid traffic base.
Paid Media
Working
Meta is delivering exceptional efficiency this week — a return on ad spend of 6.5x against a forecast of 3.0x and a cost per order of just $23 versus a forecast of $65 means every dollar is working more than twice as hard as planned.
Paid social and paid search combined are bringing in more than half of all site sessions, giving the strong conversion rate plenty of volume to work with and driving the bulk of the 106% year-on-year order growth.
Needs Attention
Google Ads is listed as not applicable this week with no data available, which is worth clarifying — if it is paused, that is a considered call, but if it is a tracking gap it should be resolved before read on paid shopping performance is lost.
The return on ad spend of 6.5x is well above forecast, which is a great problem to have, but it may signal that spend is being left on the table — there could be room to increase Meta budget while efficiency holds.
This week: Confirm whether Google Ads is intentionally paused or a reporting gap, and if paused, test reactivating a single shopping campaign to capture demand that Meta is already generating.
Retention & Revenue
Working
The BFF+ is the clear product of the moment at 413 units sold with no prior year comparison, suggesting a successful new launch that is pulling in a strong share of the 66.7% new customer mix — well above both last year and forecast.
Average order value has risen to $153 online and $160 in store, both above last year and above the $140 forecast, which points to customers either buying multiple items or trading up to newer styles.
Needs Attention
The Seeker Code reminder email on 17 August generated only $1,360 in revenue despite a healthy 54.3% open rate, suggesting the offer or landing experience after the email is not converting interested readers into buyers.
The Georgina Fox Seeker email sent to New Zealand on 18 August produced zero revenue, and while open rate at 38.4% was reasonable, the complete absence of sales from that send warrants a look at whether the NZ segment has the right product access or pricing.
This week: Review the post-click journey for the Seeker Code reminder — check whether the discount code is applying correctly at checkout and whether the landing page is matching the promise in the email.
