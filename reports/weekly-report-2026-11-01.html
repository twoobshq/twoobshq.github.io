Analyst Notes
Ecommerce & Conversion
Working
Paid social is pulling a lot of weight, accounting for 50% of all sessions and helping drive online sales to $181,069 — up 71.4% on last year — while store sales also grew strongly to $12,812, up 78%.
Average order value of $142 is tracking just above forecast at $140, and new customer acquisition at 70.5% is beating the 65% forecast, suggesting the current campaign creative is reaching genuinely new audiences.
Needs Attention
Conversion rate has dropped to 1.97% against a 3.0% forecast and last year's 2.69%, which is the clearest pressure point given sessions more than doubled but orders did not keep pace — worth checking whether landing page experience or product page load times are creating friction for the high volume of paid social visitors.
The return rate has jumped significantly to 6.3% (108 units, $14,075) compared to 3.5% and just 30 units last year, so it is worth understanding which products or sizes are being returned most before this erodes net revenue further.
This week: Pull a returns report by product and size this week to identify whether The BFF+ or The Seeker — both new or relaunched styles — are driving the spike in returns, and flag any sizing or expectation issues to address in product copy.
Paid Media
Working
Overall paid media efficiency is well ahead of target — return on ad spend of 4.1x beats the 3.0x forecast and cost per order of $35 is well under the $65 forecast, meaning the team is getting strong results from a spend level only marginally above last year ($44,579 vs $43,444).
The shift of budget toward Google — spending $7,998 this week versus only $2,120 last year — appears to be working, with the BFF and BFF+ Shopping campaigns alongside PMAX collectively driving efficient prospecting volume.
Needs Attention
Meta spend dropped to $36,581 from $41,324 last year, and while overall efficiency looks good, the heavy reliance on paid social for 50% of all site sessions means any creative fatigue or auction pressure on Meta could quickly affect overall traffic and conversion rate.
The Bottom of Funnel Search campaign ($358 spend) and Branded Search ($620) are relatively small allocations — given conversion rate is under target, it is worth checking whether retargeting and branded capture is strong enough to convert the large volume of paid social visitors who did not buy on their first visit.
This week: Review Meta creative frequency and audience overlap this week to check for fatigue, and consider reallocating a small portion of budget toward retargeting to help convert the large pool of recent non-purchasing visitors.
Retention & Revenue
Working
Email is contributing 3% of sessions (2,081 visits), which is a modest but steady owned-channel contribution on top of the paid-heavy traffic mix, and average order value continues to trend above last year at $142 versus $136.
Returning customers at 29.5% of orders are transacting at a solid rate given the strong new customer acquisition push, suggesting the existing customer base remains engaged alongside the influx of new buyers.
Needs Attention
Email's share of traffic is low relative to the volume of new customers being acquired — with 70.5% of orders this week coming from new customers, there is a significant opportunity to activate welcome and post-purchase flows to convert these buyers into repeat customers before they go cold.
Good Socks and Protecty Stuff are showing healthy unit growth versus last year, but with only 12.5 weeks of sock cover remaining, a targeted email to existing customers about bundling accessories with footwear could drive attach rate before stock tightens.
This week: Audit the welcome flow this week to confirm new customers acquired during this high-traffic period are receiving a strong post-purchase sequence, and check that the second email in the flow includes a product recommendation relevant to their first purchase.
