Analyst Notes
Ecommerce & Conversion
Working
Online sales are nearly double last year at $81,863, driven by a meaningful improvement in conversion rate (2.69% vs 1.98% last year) rather than just more traffic — sessions are only up 20% while orders are up 63%.
The BFF+ is the clear breakout product with 261 units sold this week against zero last year, and the store is also pulling its weight with sales up 89% year on year.
Needs Attention
Paid Social dominates traffic at 47% of sessions, which means the site is heavily dependent on one channel — organic search (14%), direct (13%), and email (5%) are all comparatively thin and would need to work much harder if paid spend pulled back.
Conversion rate at 2.69% is tracking below the 3.0% forecast, so there is revenue being left on the table even with strong traffic — worth understanding whether a specific channel or device is dragging the average down.
This week: Pull a conversion rate breakdown by traffic channel this week to identify which source is underconverting and flag it for targeted page or offer testing.
Paid Media
Working
Overall paid media is delivering a return on ad spend of 4.3x against a forecast of 3.0x, with a cost per order of $35 versus a forecast of $65 — the budget increase of 50% year on year is paying off well above expectations.
Meta is carrying the bulk of spend at $14,694 and generating the lion's share of paid sessions through Paid Social, which at 9,622 sessions is by far the largest single traffic source on the site.
Needs Attention
Google spend of $4,205 is spread across three non-branded shopping and Performance Max campaigns with no branded campaign visible, so there is a risk that brand search demand generated by Meta activity is not being captured efficiently.
The BFF & BFF+ Shopping campaign at $1,306 and the general AU Shopping campaign at $1,492 are running alongside a Performance Max campaign at $1,407, and without visibility on individual returns it is difficult to know whether all three are pulling in the same direction or cannibalising each other.
This week: Check whether a branded Google Search campaign is active this week, and if not, set one up immediately to capture the brand interest that Meta spend is likely generating.
Retention & Revenue
Working
The single email campaign sent this week — the BFF holiday send — achieved a 56.5% open rate, which is a strong result and shows the list is engaged with product-led storytelling.
Average order value online has grown to $150 versus $128 last year and is ahead of the $140 forecast, suggesting returning customers in particular are spending more per visit.
Needs Attention
Email drove only 986 sessions this week (5% of total), which is low relative to the size of activity the brand appears to be running — with one campaign sent, there may be an opportunity to send more frequently given the strong open rate signal.
New customers make up 66.7% of orders this week, which is healthy for acquisition but means retention flows (welcome series, post-purchase) are under pressure to convert a large new cohort into repeat buyers over the coming weeks.
This week: Schedule at least one additional email to the existing customer segment this week to capitalise on the strong open rate and begin converting the influx of new customers before they go cold.
